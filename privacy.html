Privacy Policy for Cable Core

Last Updated: August 27, 2026

1. Overview

Cable Core is a tool for cable technicians to manage site data and project progress. We prioritize your privacy and minimize data collection by storing most data locally on your device.

2. Data Collection and Usage

  • Personal Information: We store your Name, Email, and Employee ID locally to personalize reports and identify your profile in team sync folders.
  • Photos: The app accesses your Camera and Storage to save safety photos, certification images, and site progress. These are stored locally and only uploaded if you enable OneDrive Sync.
  • Location: We use your device's location to tag work sites for accurate reporting.
  • App Activity: We log interaction data (Activity Entries) to help you track your work progress.

3. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

Your data is primarily stored in a local database on your device. We use the following third-party services:

  • Microsoft OneDrive/MSAL: If you use the Sync feature, your data is transmitted to Microsoft servers. This is governed by Microsoft's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing: For Pro subscriptions, payment information is processed by Google, not by us.

4. Permissions

The app requires the following permissions to function:

  • Camera: To take site and safety photos.
  • Location: To identify site locations.
  • Storage: To save reports and project data.
  • Internet: To sync data with OneDrive and verify subscriptions.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can delete all local data by clearing the app's storage in your device settings. For instructions on removing cloud-synced data, please visit our Data Deletion Page.
