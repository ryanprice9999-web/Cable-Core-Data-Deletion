Cable Core Data Deletion

At Cable Core, we respect your privacy and provide transparent ways to manage and delete your data. Because our app uses a combination of local storage and cloud synchronization, please follow the steps below to ensure your data is fully removed.

1. Local App Data

Most of your project data, including site details, cable runs, and your technician profile, is stored locally on your device. To delete this data:

  • Open Android Settings.
  • Go to Apps > Cable Core.
  • Select Storage and tap Clear Data.
  • This will permanently remove all local databases and images from your device.

2. Microsoft OneDrive Synchronization

If you linked the app to your Microsoft OneDrive account, your project files and profile information may be stored in your company's designated sync folder. To remove this:

  • Log into your Microsoft Account Security Dashboard.
  • Navigate to Apps and Services you've given access.
  • Find "Cable Core" and select Remove these permissions.
  • Manually delete the "CableCore_Sync" or designated team folder from your OneDrive storage.

3. Request Manual Deletion

If you have joined a Team and wish to have your profile and activity entries removed from the Team Leader's records, or if you need assistance with any other data removal, please contact us:

Email: [Coreconstructs@outlook.com]

Please include "Data Deletion Request" in the subject line. We will respond within 48-72 hours.
